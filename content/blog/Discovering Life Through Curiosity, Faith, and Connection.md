### **A Curious Life: Wearing Many Hats**

I wear many hats in life—[broadcaster](https://faisalqureshi.com/tv-anchor-perks-lessons-beyond-the-spotlight/ "TV Anchor Perks: Lessons Beyond the Spotlight"), personal development guide, entrepreneur, engineer, software developer, traveler, and lifelong learner. Some might call me a jack of all trades, but I see myself as someone endlessly curious. My interests grow every day, and I love exploring new ideas.

Life has been generous enough to offer countless opportunities, and I’ve worked hard to make the most of them. Each experience—whether success or failure—has contributed to who I am today.

### **A Life Guided by Faith**

One constant in my life is my faith. I let God guide my decisions, big and small. Looking back, this has been one of the best choices I ever made. It gave me strength during tough times and gratitude during good ones.

Life threw its fair share of challenges my way, but those moments taught me valuable lessons. Now, in this later phase of life, I focus on undoing past mistakes and creating meaningful energy for the future.

### **Born in Pakistan, Shaped by International Education**

I was born, raised, and have lived most of my life in Pakistan. But I’ve had the privilege of pursuing **an international education** and traveling extensively. These experiences opened my eyes to different cultures, perspectives, and ways of thinking.

Through these journeys, I became more tolerant of others’ views and learned to appreciate diversity in all its forms. This openness has stayed with me and continues to influence how I interact with the world.

### **A Multifaceted Journey: From Engineering to Broadcasting**

My career path reflects my diverse interests. I’m an electrical engineer by education, an entrepreneur by profession, and a broadcaster by passion. Oh, and did I mention? I’m also a self-taught software developer. Running my own software business while hosting TV shows kept me busy for years—but it was worth every moment.

These roles may seem unrelated, but they all stem from my curiosity and desire to explore new horizons. Together, they’ve shaped who I am today.

### **Speaking to the Masses: A Transformative Experience**

Becoming a TV celebrity opened doors I never expected. One of the most impactful was being invited as a public speaker. Universities, conferences, corporate meetings—you name it. I found myself addressing diverse audiences, sometimes more than a thousand people at a time.

Speaking to such varied groups taught me how to connect with people on a deeper level. It wasn’t just about sharing ideas—it was about listening, understanding, and adapting my message to resonate with different perspectives. This experience transformed the way I communicate and interact with others.

To read more about how public speaking changed me, check out my blog post: **[How Public Speaking Transformed My Perspective](https://faisalqureshi.com/how-public-speaking-transformed-my-perspective/ "How Public Speaking Transformed My Perspective")**.

### **Sharing What I’ve Learned**

Over the years, I’ve accumulated lessons—not just through books or formal education, but through real-life experiences. Both the highs and lows have humbly contributed to shaping who I am.

In recent years, I’ve felt a strong urge to share these lessons with others. Why? Because if even one person learns from my mistakes or finds inspiration in my journey, then sharing my story becomes worthwhile.

This website is where I pour out my thoughts. My [blog](https://faisalqureshi.com/blog/ "The Blog") covers everything under the sun—inspirations, reflections, questions, answers, and yes, loose ends. If something catches my interest, I write about it. Think of it as an open notebook filled with musings, discoveries, and insights.

### **A Boomer Reflecting on Life’s Purpose**

As a boomer, I find myself reflecting more deeply these days. I’m no longer chasing material success or external validation. Instead, I want to leave behind a legacy of kindness, wisdom, and **continuous learning** .

I spend a lot of time reading and seeking information, which I try to turn into knowledge based on all else that I learn and know, with an objective, unbiased mind. There’s so much to explore, and this pursuit keeps me engaged and growing.
