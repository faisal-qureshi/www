> [!abstract] In short
> Words flow when I talk. They stall when I type. This is how I'm making peace with both.

## The hook

Ask me a question and I will answer for ten minutes. Ask me to write that answer down and I will stare at a blinking cursor.

## Background

Nearly two decades on television trained me to think out loud. The camera became a collaborator. The keyboard never did. See [[me/the-broadcaster|The Broadcaster]] for the background.

## The main idea

### Speaking is immediate

A video captures tone, pauses and warmth. A paragraph needs all that rebuilt from scratch.

### Writing is durable

Text can be searched, linked and revisited, which is why this site exists. A video becomes more useful once it has a page like [[videos/conversation-on-freedom|this one]].

### The hybrid workflow

> [!tip] My current process
> 1. Record the idea as a video.
> 2. Transcribe it.
> 3. Edit the transcript into a blog post or [[microblog/2026-10-11-record-first|a microblog]].

## What I learned

- Don't fight your strongest medium.
- Edit ruthlessly after the first draft.
- A written summary makes a video 10x more discoverable.

## Conclusion

I'll keep talking. I'll just learn to write down what I said.
