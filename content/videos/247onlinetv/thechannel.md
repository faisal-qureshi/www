## Back in 2012

I was at the peak of my TV career. However, I was sure that mainstream TV was about done, and social media TV, or new media, as it was called back then, will completely take over.

Determined to be a first mover, at least in my own region, I launched Pakistan's one and only organized YouTube TV Channel, called 247OnlineTV.

Turned out I was way ahead of my time. The revolution did happen - but much later.

I eventually lost #interest in running the channel, since this is long before you could monetize YouTube videos.

The channel still exists, and can be watched at [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@247onlinetv4)
