### When love runs deeper than reasons, words tremble. This is what happens when the heart overflows without explanation.

![[Blog/Musings/i-love-you-and-i-dont-need-a-reason-why-1793002285331-faisalqureshi-com.webp|700x393]]

Today—\
I want to drown you in joy,\
pour it over you like sunlight,\
more than ever, more than my bones can hold.

I know exactly how to love you,\
but the _why_?\
The why doesn’t matter.\
It just _is_.

Listen—\
I don’t need dreams if I have your breath against my skin.\
I don’t need purpose if I have your hands to hold.\
_You_ are the dream that came true.

And today, you look at me,\
and you _see_ it—\
how you’ve split me open,\
how I’m not the same person anymore.\
I’m softer.\
I’m louder.\
I’m a firework barely contained.

I could lie, try to be something grand—\
but the truth is simpler:\
I am love, swollen and raw.\
I am love, aching and alive.\
I am love, shaking apart just to stay near you.

Today, I swear—\
every wish you whisper, I’ll turn to gold.\
Every fear, I’ll dissolve.\
Because today, I woke up and _knew_—\
I am yours.\
Fully.\
Without apology.

If you stand beside me,\
I’ll shout it to the sky:\
_This is what love does._\
It ruins you.\
It rebuilds you.\
It leaves you speechless,\
because no words are big enough.

So please—\
_listen_.\
After all this waiting,\
after all this breaking,\
after every unanswered prayer—\
here it is.\
My heart.\
My everything.\
All for you.\
_Only you._
