### Sometimes tears fall not from pain but from gratitude—for loving deeply and being loved in return.

![[Blog/Musings/sometimes-when-youre-crying-youre-happy-1793007307066-faisalqureshi-com.webp|700x391]]

Sometimes tears fall,\
Not from sorrow, but joy untold.

I cry for you, my love,\
Yet happiness blooms where pain dissolves.

Grateful I am—to miss someone so true,\
A heart like yours—pure and eternal too.

Glad we were close, even briefly,\
Your laughter lingers, your touch still frees me.

In my heart, you’ll forever stay,\
A treasure untouched by decay.

Happy I am—that I once knew you,\
Happier still—if fate renews what we do.
