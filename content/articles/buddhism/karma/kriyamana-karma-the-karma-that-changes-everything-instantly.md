## **What Is Kriyamana Karma?**

Kriyamana Karma is the karma you create in the present moment, shaping your immediate reality. The term “kriyamana” means “being done” or “being performed,” highlighting its active and dynamic nature.

At first glance, Kriyamana Karma and [Agami Karma](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now) can seem similar—they both involve actions you take in the present moment. However, their focus and impact differ significantly.

Kriyamana Karma centers on the _here and now_ —the instant effects of your thoughts, words, and actions. It’s about the energetic ripples you create in the present moment.

In contrast, [Agami Karma](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now) looks ahead—it’s about the future consequences of your actions. While Kriyamana Karma shapes your current reality, [Agami Karma](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now) plants seeds that will grow into tomorrow’s outcomes.

To clarify the distinction, consider these examples:

- **[Agami Karma](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now)** : If you start saving money today, the benefits will come months or years from now. It’s about actions that shape your future.
- **Kriyamana Karma** : If you smile at someone today, you might brighten their day right now. It’s about the immediate effects of your actions.

Every action you take creates an energetic imprint, influencing your environment, relationships, and inner state in real time. This is the essence of Kriyamana Karma—**it’s not about what will happen later but how your choices affect the present moment**.

---

## **How Kriyamana Karma Works**

Kriyamana Karma is the karma you create right now, in every action, thought, and word. It’s not about what happened in the past or what might happen in the future—it’s about the energy you’re putting out into the world at this very moment. Unlike [Sanchita Karma](sanchita-karma-a-simple-guide-to-understanding-your-past) (accumulated past karma) or [Prarabdha Karma](prarabdha-karma-how-to-handle-your-current-life-dynamics) (your life script), Kriyamana Karma is completely under your control. Every choice you make shapes your present reality.

To illustrate this, imagine throwing a stone into a pond:

- The act of throwing the stone is your Kriyamana Karma .
- The ripples spreading outward are the immediate consequences of your action.

Although these ripples might seem small, they affect the entire pond—just as your actions influence your current reality. For example:

- A kind word spoken creates warmth and connection instantly.
- An act of anger generates tension and disrupts harmony in the moment.

Kriyamana Karma shows us that our actions don’t just shape the future—they also influence how we feel, how others respond to us, and the quality of our immediate experience.

---

## **The Immediate Impact**

The beauty of Kriyamana Karma lies in its immediacy. It reminds us that every action has a direct effect on our present reality. Consider these examples:

- A kind word: Immediately fosters warmth and connection.
- An act of anger: Instantly creates tension and disrupts harmony.
- A moment of meditation: Brings peace and clarity to your mind right away.
- A selfless act of service: Generates feelings of fulfillment and interconnectedness in the moment.

These examples illustrate how Kriyamana Karma isn’t just about long-term consequences—it’s about the energetic imprint you leave on the present. This imprint shapes your environment, your relationships, and your inner state.

---

## **Taking Conscious Action**

Understanding Kriyamana Karma empowers you to take responsibility and control of your immediate reality. You’re not just a passive recipient of your past karma—you’re an active participant in shaping your present and future through your choices in each moment.

This understanding encourages you to:

- Cultivate mindfulness: Pay attention to your thoughts, words, and actions in the present moment.
- Act with intention: Be aware of the motivations behind your actions.
- Choose positivity: Strive to cultivate thoughts, words, and deeds that promote well-being and harmony.
- Learn from consequences: Observe how your actions affect your present experience and adjust accordingly.

---

## **Living Intentionally**

Kriyamana Karma invites you to live with greater awareness. Ask yourself:

- What kind of energy am I creating in this moment?
- Are my actions aligned with the reality I want to experience right now?
- Am I taking responsibility for the immediate impact of my choices?

By reflecting on these questions, you move from reacting to life’s challenges to responding with purpose. You become the architect of your immediate reality, steering it toward growth, harmony, and fulfillment.

---

## **The Link to Agami Karma**

While Kriyamana Karma has immediate effects, it’s also the foundation of your [Agami Karma](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now). The seeds you sow in the present (Kriyamana) will eventually sprout and bear fruit in the future ([Agami](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now)).

For instance:

- Practicing kindness today plants seeds for positive future experiences.
- Reacting with anger sows seeds that may lead to challenges ahead.

By being mindful of your Kriyamana Karma, you can actively shape a better [Agami Karma](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now). Every decision you make influences what happens next, creating momentum for favorable outcomes. Ultimately, your actions today determine the possibilities of tomorrow.

---

## **In Conclusion**

Kriyamana Karma reminds us that the present moment is where you actively mold your reality. Every thought, word, and action shapes the life you’re living right now—and lays the groundwork for your future. By embracing this principle, you take ownership of your life, cultivate positive change, and consciously create a brighter today and tomorrow.

**Your future isn’t something that happens to you—it’s something you create. Ultimately, the choices you make today have immediate effects, influence your long-term path, and ripple through the eternal cycles of your soul’s journey.**

To better understand this flow, explore the four kinds of karma—[Sanchita](sanchita-karma-a-simple-guide-to-understanding-your-past), [Prarabdha](prarabdha-karma-how-to-handle-your-current-life-dynamics), [Agami](agami-karma-the-power-to-create-your-future-now), and Kriyamana. Each plays a unique role in shaping your existence, offering deeper insight into how karma influences your life across lifetimes. By learning about these dynamics, you can navigate your journey with greater awareness and intention.
