Hello. Thank you very much for stopping by.

[I am](/index) an electrical engineer by training, though I never spent a day doing engineering. I was just lucky enough to say yes to every new opportunity life sent my way.

In the 1990s, I taught myself to write software and started a business. I became a tech entrepreneur.

Then life took a wonderful turn. In addition to my software business, I was offered the chance to become a TV host, which I accepted without hesitation. I started by talking about social issues before moving into journalism.

The fame and financial freedom gave me the opportunity to speak on stages about life, love, and our nation. Somehow, I became an influencer decades before social media influencing was a thing.

My biggest dream was simple: to retire early and just enjoy life. By God's grace, I reached that goal in my 50s. I walked away completely and embraced my freedom. The freedom to do exactly what I want, when I want to do it.

I tried to keep writing, but when you have spent years in front of the camera, talking feels much more natural. So, I still blog occasionally, but I vlog much more often. I am sure you will enjoy my work linked from my social media page.

Sharing my ideas is wonderful, but connecting with people is even better. So, please take a moment to drop a quick note on my contact page. I would love to hear from you.
