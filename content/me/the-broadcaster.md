> [!abstract] In one line
> Words are my tools. On screen, on the page or on camera, the goal is connection.

## 📺 The TV Anchor

I had the privilege of being among Pakistan's **first generation of TV talk show hosts**. I appeared on screen for nearly two decades, long before television became the crowded space it is today.

I hosted shows in both English and Urdu. My programmes offered thoughtful analysis of current affairs, nuanced discussions of social issues, and a voice for perspectives that are often overlooked.

## ✍️ The Writer

This blog is my intellectual home. Here I explore faith, relationships, personal growth and unconventional perspectives.

I write not as someone who has all the answers, but as a fellow traveller trying to make sense of life's complexities. Above all, I want my words to start meaningful conversations. 

## 🎤 The Speaker

I have a lot to say, and I find it far easier to speak than to write. Words flow naturally when I'm talking, and years as a TV host made me comfortable in front of a camera.

Whether it's a thought, an idea or a lesson, I often prefer to record a video and share it with the world. It is immediate, personal and unfiltered.

> [!quote]
> Speaking isn't just about sharing. It's about connecting. If my words resonate with even one person, the effort is worth it.
