## ☪️ The Muslim by Conscious Choice

I was born into Islam, but that is not why I am a Muslim today. My faith is the result of conscious exploration.

I took a journey through all the major religious traditions. After that exploration, I came to see Islam as the divine culmination, the final and complete message from God.

Islam is the foundation of my spiritual life. At the same time, I continue to explore other faiths, philosophies and ways of life, because I want to understand what connects us all.

> [!note] A gentle word
> Some Westerners and Islamophobes may feel troubled by this belief. My intention isn't to provoke. I simply hope they will take the time to understand Islam beyond the stereotypes.

## 📚 The Eternal Student

The idea of a **bodhisattva**, a seeker who strives for enlightenment not only for themselves but to help others, resonates deeply with me. It fuels my unquenchable thirst for knowledge.

For me, learning is more than acquiring information. It is transformation. First I focus on growing myself. Then, through compassion, I share what I have learned with others searching for wisdom.

This cycle of growth and giving back is what makes the journey meaningful.

## 🎓 The Committed Teacher

Knowledge only becomes valuable when it is shared. The Buddhist concept of **bodhichitta** drives me to teach everything I learn.

Whether through writing, speaking or personal mentoring, I believe wisdom multiplies when it is given away. Teaching also solidifies my own understanding while contributing to someone else's growth.
