Hello. I am Faisal Qureshi, an entrepreneur, engineer, software developer, mainstream TV host, social-media broadcaster, and retired from most of it.

I'm originally from Pakistan, but being retired gives me the freedom to be wherever I want to be, and do whatever I want to do. Within reason, of course.

I believe in doing nothing. Or at least nothing of significance. I believe in just living as best as one can, and to leave behind whatever one picks up along the way.

I have a high IQ problem. I am the founding chairperson of Mensa Pakistan. Even though it is nothing significant these days, but it does reflect on the chaotic state, and the painful analytical capability, of my mind. It is a blessing and a curse at the same time.

This "digital garden" or "mindmap", is hopefully my returning offer to this life. Over the years I have made many attempts at archiving everything I know or believe, but have always ended up starting again (more about that somewhere in this garden). However, I feel, and hope, that this effort will stick. So help me god.
