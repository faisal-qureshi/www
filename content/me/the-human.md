> Money is a tool, change is growth, feelings are the point, and I'm still not finished.

## 🕊️ The Purposeful Retiree

I retired after an important realisation: **money is merely a tool for living, not the purpose of living.**

Once I reached financial independence, everything changed. I gained the freedom to spend my time on what fulfils me instead of what pays me.

Admittedly, this freedom came later than I had hoped. But now each day brings the luxury of pursuing what truly matters.

## ♻️ The Constant Reinventor

Change has been the one constant in my life. I have never been content staying in a single role or identity.

I went from engineer to entrepreneur, from broadcaster to writer, and from seeker to teacher. Each evolution builds on what came before while opening new dimensions of understanding.

Instead of seeing transformation as disruption, I see it as natural growth.

## 💘 The Unabashed Romantic

I wholeheartedly embrace the full emotional spectrum of life: the highs, the lows and everything in between.

Whether it's the exhilaration of falling in love, the ache of heartbreak or the quiet joy of a deep connection, I see these emotions not as weaknesses but as the essence of being human.

Love, in all its forms, shapes how I experience the world: my interactions with people, my appreciation of life and my understanding of its beauty. Passion drives me to seek meaningful connections and to cherish the moments that make life profound.

This romantic sensibility isn't only about romance. It is about celebrating the depth of feeling that binds us all together.

## 🚧 The Perpetual Work-in-Progress

Fragments of my past thinking are scattered across the internet, but I'm less concerned with compiling them than with continuing to grow.

The present moment always offers new opportunities for understanding, while the past remains useful mainly for its lessons.
