## 🔌 The Electrical Engineer

My journey began with a degree in Electrical Engineering. It gave me a structured, analytical way to solve problems. I never worked as an electrical engineer, but that training shaped how I think.

Even today I break complex systems into understandable components, whether the system is software or an abstract idea. That one skill has served me well in every area of life.

## 💾 The Self-Taught Technology Entrepreneur

I ran a software company in Pakistan for nearly **20 years**, back when hardly anyone was doing it. I had no fancy degrees. I simply learned by doing and figured things out as I went.

That hands-on experience taught me resilience and adaptability, two qualities that have been invaluable throughout my career.

## 🧠 The Mensan

I had the opportunity to set up the Pakistani chapter of Mensa International and served as its **founding chairperson**.

One side effect of a high IQ is that I can see things from perspectives most people overlook, and I have come to deeply value that ability.

I have also learned that intellectual rigour must go hand in hand with humility and compassion.

> [!quote]
> True wisdom isn't about knowing more. It's about using what we know to uplift others.
